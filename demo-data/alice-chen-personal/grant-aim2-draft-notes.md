# Grant Aim 2 Draft Notes — NIH R01 Resubmission

Aim 2: "Test whether multimodal language-vision training objectives align artificial and biological visual representations"

## What the reviewer said last time
"Aim 2 is underpowered. 8 subjects is insufficient for the proposed cross-subject analyses given the effect sizes in the literature."

## My response plan
- Add power analysis: based on our pilot data (r=0.61, 8 subjects), power for detecting r>0.4 at α=0.05 is 91%. We're adequately powered for the primary question.
- Expand to 16 subjects (add 8 in years 2–3)
- Add EEG component for temporal resolution (cheaper than fMRI for the extra subjects)

## New framing for Aim 2
Lead with the clinical translation angle: "Understanding what makes artificial systems match biological visual processing has direct implications for designing BCIs that interface with visual cortex." This ties to Clara's work and makes Aim 2 feel less academic.

## Key citations to add
- Scotti et al. 2024 (MindEye2) — shows alignment works for decoding
- Schneider et al. 2025 (contrastive neuro) — session-invariant representations
- Kriegeskorte 2015 (deep neural networks as models of visual cortex)
