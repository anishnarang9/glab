# Podcast Notes — Huberman Lab (the one about sleep)

Listened during the run on May 10. (~90 min)

**Main points I'll actually remember:**
- 10 min of outdoor light in the morning → big effect on circadian rhythm. I already knew this but I actually don't do it.
- Avoid bright light 10pm-4am. I look at my laptop in bed until 1am. Classic.
- Temperature drop by 1-3°F needed to fall asleep. Cold room = better sleep.

**Points I'm skeptical about:**
- The specific supplement stack recommendations feel like sponsored content. Noted.
- The alpha-GPC recommendation — not enough evidence for healthy people.

**Relevance to my life:**
I sleep badly during scanning weeks because I'm anxious about data quality. This is anxiety, not circadian rhythm. No podcast will fix it.

The cold room thing I'll actually try. Set thermostat to 67°F tonight.

**Unrelated:** Huberman's voice is extremely soothing for running. Good pace-setter.
