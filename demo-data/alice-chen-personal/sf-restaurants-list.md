# SF Restaurants to Try

Places people have mentioned that I keep forgetting:

**Dinner**
- Nopa — open late, good for after seminar drinks. Mission-ish.
- Birdsong — supposedly incredible tasting menu. Save for a special occasion / visitor
- Nopalito — Rachel went last week, said the mole is amazing

**Lunch near campus**
- The place on Irving with the banh mi — I went once 2 years ago and still think about it. What is it called??
- Sushirrito — Mei from the lab loves this. Seems chaotic but worth trying

**Coffee**
- Sightglass (SOMA) — good for working remotely on analysis days
- Verve on Market — passed it yesterday, smelled incredible

**Note:** stop eating at the hospital cafeteria every day. It's bad for morale.
