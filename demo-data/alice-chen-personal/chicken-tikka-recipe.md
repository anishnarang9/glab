# Chicken Tikka Masala — Kenji Recipe (modified)

Made this last Sunday. Finally got it right. Writing it down before I forget what I changed.

## Marinade (do night before)
- 1.5 lbs chicken thighs, cut into 2in pieces
- 1 cup whole milk yogurt
- 2 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp kashmiri chili (KEY — the red color and mild heat)
- 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- Salt

Marinate overnight minimum.

## Sauce
- 2 tbsp butter + 1 tbsp neutral oil
- 1 large onion, thinly sliced — cook low and slow for 20 min until caramelized (don't rush this)
- 4 garlic cloves + 1in ginger, grated
- 1 can whole tomatoes (28oz) — crush by hand
- 1 tsp each: cumin, coriander, garam masala
- 1/2 tsp kashmiri chili
- 3/4 cup heavy cream
- Fenugreek leaves at the end (kasuri methi — DO NOT SKIP, this is what makes it taste like restaurant)

## What changed from last time
- Used chicken thighs instead of breast — much better
- The caramelized onion instead of raw blended onions is the big difference

## Notes
Serves 4. Good with basmati and naan. Even better the next day.
