# Hypothesis: L4 PV+ Interneurons Are Near the AI/SI Transition

## Setup
Bob's finding: PV+ E/I ratio = 1.9 (corrected: ~2.3 after recall correction). Brunel 2000 predicts the AI/SI transition at g ≈ 0.5, corresponding to E/I ≈ 2.0.

Bob's PV+ population is sitting right at the transition boundary.

## Hypothesis
L4 in mouse V1 operates near a synchronous transition. Visual stimulation pushes the network into the synchronous regime briefly, generating transient gamma oscillations (30-80Hz). This gamma is not epiphenomenal — it reflects the near-critical state that maximizes dynamic range for visual processing.

## Predictions
1. LFP recordings from Bob's tissue block (if available) should show gamma power that increases with visual stimulus contrast
2. The gamma frequency should match the oscillatory frequency predicted by Brunel for their E/I ratio (~45Hz at E/I=2.3)
3. Optogenetic suppression of PV+ cells should reduce gamma and *reduce* visual decoding accuracy (because it pushes the network away from criticality)

## Test
Predictions 1 & 2 are potentially testable from existing data (Bob has LFP channels on his MEA during calcium imaging). Prediction 3 requires a new experiment.

## Connection to Alice's work
If visual cortex near criticality maximizes dynamic range → should maximize decoding accuracy. If we could manipulate E/I ratio pharmacologically and test Alice's decoding... that's a whole separate experiment but a good grant angle.
