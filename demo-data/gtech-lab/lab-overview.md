# GTech Neuroscience Lab — Overview

## Mission

GTech Neuroscience Lab investigates the computational principles underlying perception, cognition, and motor control in the mammalian brain. We combine large-scale neural recording, electron microscopy, brain-computer interfaces, and theoretical modeling to build a unified picture of how neural circuits give rise to behavior.

Founded in 2019 at Georgia Tech, the lab sits at the intersection of neuroscience, machine learning, and biomedical engineering. We believe the next decade of neuroscience will be defined by labs that can operate across scales — from synaptic ultrastructure to whole-brain dynamics — and we are building the infrastructure to do exactly that.
